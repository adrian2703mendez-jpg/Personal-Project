Description

Welcome to Seeds of Wealth, a course preparing future generations to be financially stable and liberate — able to manage their own finances and incomes with ease and confidence. Seeds of Wealth (SoW) is an investing course specially designed for young adults and teens to manage and achieve their dreamed financial future. It teaches you how to invest, save, budget, and make your money work for you. The course offers a variety of tools and resources to learn the art of investing like real-life examples, interactive activities, and a community of like-minded individuals on the journey to financial stability.

Course Features

The course offers a systematic way to study and learn the basics and advanced investing. Most of the course is designed to be interactive and engaging, supporting self-paced learning. The course is divided into several modules, each covering a different aspect of investing.

The modules are:

  • Introduction to Investing
  • Understanding the Stock Market
  • Investment Strategies
  • Risk Management
  • Building a Portfolio
  • Financial Planning and Budgeting
  • Retirement Planning
  • Real Estate Investing
  • Cryptocurrency and Digital Assets
